Privacy Policy

Photo Spec is local-first. Photos you drop, pick, or paste are processed only in this browser with the canvas API. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. There is no account and no cloud backup.

The optional applicant profile (name, date of birth, phone, email, address, parent names, national ID note) is stored only in this browser’s localStorage under the key photo-spec:profile:v1. Copy buttons write to your clipboard on this device. This is a cheat-sheet, not autofill of any government or exam website.

Language preference is stored as ps_lang in localStorage. Last-used preset settings may also stay in localStorage.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

You can clear the profile in the app, or clear site data in your browser settings.

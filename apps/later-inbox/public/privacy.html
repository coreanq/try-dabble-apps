Privacy Policy

Later Inbox is local-first. Links, titles, why-notes, pins, and status are stored only in this browser (localStorage key later-inbox:items:v1). Language may also use localStorage (li_lang). Nothing is uploaded to our servers for accounts or backup.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server-side stash of your reading list.

The optional clipboard paste button reads the clipboard only after you tap it, in this browser, to fill the URL field. Bookmark HTML and JSON import stay on this device.

Advertising: Google AdSense may be active on this site. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure performance. See Google’s advertising policies and your browser or Google account settings to manage ad cookies.

You can export or delete everything in the app, or clear site data in your browser settings.

Privacy Policy

Later Inbox is local-first. Links, titles, why-notes, pins, and status are stored only in this browser (localStorage key later-inbox:items:v1). Language may also use localStorage (later-inbox:lang). Nothing is uploaded to our servers for accounts or backup.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server-side stash of your reading list.

The optional clipboard paste button reads the clipboard only after you tap it, in this browser, to fill the URL field. Bookmark HTML and JSON import stay on this device.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

You can export or delete everything in the app, or clear site data in your browser settings.

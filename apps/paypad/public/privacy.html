Privacy Policy

Paypad is local-first. Your paycheck plans, earnings, envelopes and preferences stay in this browser on this device. Language uses this browser and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you enter is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no bank or card connection, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your budget. The app never fetches anything from the internet beyond its own files.

Backups: “Export JSON” creates a file on your device through the browser’s download. Where you keep that file is up to you; the app never sends it anywhere.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads, no in-app purchases, no Plus tier and no subscription of its own. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Use “Delete everything” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.

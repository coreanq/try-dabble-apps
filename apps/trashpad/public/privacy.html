Privacy Policy

Trashpad is local-first. Note text and each note's delete timer are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys trashpad:notes:v1 and trashpad:default-ttl:v1). Language uses localStorage (trashpad:lang) and a td_lang cookie shared across try-dabble.com subdomains. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no subscription, and no server-side copy of your notes. There is no sharing and no burn-after-reading link.

Every note is deleted once its timer runs out, counted from your last edit. Deletion happens in this browser. There is no archive and no undo.

Advertising: Google AdSense may be active on this site. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure performance. See Google’s advertising policies and your browser or Google account settings to manage ad cookies.

You can trash any note in the app, or clear site data in your browser settings.

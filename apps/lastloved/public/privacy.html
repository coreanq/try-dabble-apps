Privacy Policy

Lastloved is local-first. The songs you add — the title, the artist, the date you last loved it, the number of years, and how many times it has come back — are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys lastloved:songs:v1 and lastloved:years:v1). Language uses localStorage (lastloved:lang) and a td_lang cookie shared across try-dabble.com subdomains. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no payment step, and no server-side copy of your shelf. Lastloved does not connect to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube or any other streaming service, does not read a music library, does not ask for files, and never plays audio. A song is just a title and an artist that you typed.

Nothing expires on a timer. The number of years you choose is when a song comes back to the top of your list — never when it is deleted. An entry is removed only when you remove it, or when you clear site data in your browser.

Advertising: Google AdSense may be active on this site. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure performance. See Google’s advertising policies and your browser or Google account settings to manage ad cookies.

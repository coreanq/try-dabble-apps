Privacy Policy

Scanprice is local-first. The barcodes you scan, the product names, the prices, the store tags and the dates they were seen are stored only in this browser (localStorage key scanprice:items:v1). Language uses localStorage (scanprice:lang) and a td_lang cookie shared across try-dabble.com subdomains. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

The camera. When you open the scan window the page asks your browser for the back camera and looks at the frames for a barcode using the browser's own barcode reader. The video never leaves the page, no frame is recorded, and nothing is sent anywhere. If you read a barcode from a photo instead, that photo is decoded in this tab and is not uploaded either. Closing the scan window stops the camera.

There are no accounts of any kind, no sign-in, no payment step, and no server-side copy of your prices. There is no crowd-sourced price feed, no retailer API and no price sharing — the only prices here are the ones you typed.

Export is local too. The JSON and CSV files are built inside this page and handed straight to your browser's download — they are never sent anywhere first. A JSON import is read in this page with the browser's own file reader; the file is not uploaded.

Nothing expires on a timer. A code or a price row is removed only when you remove it, or when you clear site data in your browser.

Advertising: Google AdSense may be active on this site. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure performance. See Google’s advertising policies and your browser or Google account settings to manage ad cookies.

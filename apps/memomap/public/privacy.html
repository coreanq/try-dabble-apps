Privacy Policy

Memomap is local-first. Pin coordinates and memo text are stored only in this browser (localStorage key memomap:pins:v1, plus memomap:view:v1 for the last map position). Attached photos are stored only in this device's IndexedDB database memomap. Language uses localStorage (memomap:lang) and a td_lang cookie shared across try-dabble.com subdomains. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no subscription, and no server-side copy of your pins or photos. Pins are private by default; there is no sharing and no public profile.

Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org). Requesting a tile tells that service which map area is being viewed, as any web map does. Your pins, memos and photos are never sent with those requests.

Nothing expires on a timer and an app update never clears your pins. A pin is removed only when you delete it, or when you clear site data in your browser.

Privacy Policy

Peptidelog is local-first. Your compounds, vials, dose logs, schedules, supplies and preferences (syringe scale, default site, text size, half-life toggle) are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys peptidelog:compounds:v1, peptidelog:vials:v1, peptidelog:logs:v1, peptidelog:schedules:v1, peptidelog:supplies:v1 and peptidelog:prefs:v1). Language uses localStorage (peptidelog:lang) and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you enter is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your logs. The app makes no network request other than loading its own files.

Not medical advice: the app stores what you type and does arithmetic on it. It does not recommend doses, has no AI assistant and no clinician features. Discuss dosing with a qualified clinician.

Backups: “Export JSON” creates a file on your device through the browser’s download. Where you keep that file is up to you; the app never sends it anywhere.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Use “Delete all data” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.

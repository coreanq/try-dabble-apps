Privacy Policy

PetPlay is local-first. Your pet's look (species, parts, colours and, if you chose one, the photo sprite), its name, its needs meters and your preferences are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys petplay:pet:v1, petplay:needs:v1 and petplay:prefs:v1). Language uses localStorage (petplay:lang) and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Photos: when you use “Use my photo”, the picture is opened, cropped and tinted by your browser on this device and saved as a small image inside the pet record. It is never uploaded, never sent to any image or AI service, and the app contains no generative AI of any kind.

Nothing you enter is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your pet. The app makes no network request other than loading its own files.

Backups: “Export JSON” creates a file on your device through the browser’s download. Where you keep that file is up to you; the app never sends it anywhere.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads, no in-app purchases, no tokens and no subscription. Every part and colour is free. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Use “Delete all” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.

Privacy Policy

Lockpad is local-first. Your notes (title, body, tags, colour), the auto-lock preferences and the wrong-PIN backoff counters stay in this browser on this device. Language uses this browser and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you enter is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your notes. The app never knows what you wrote beyond what stays on this device.

Locked notes: when you lock a note, its body is encrypted on this device with the browser's Web Crypto API (PBKDF2-SHA-256 key derivation, AES-GCM encryption) and only the ciphertext, a random salt, the iv and the KDF parameters are stored. The PIN itself is never stored. A decrypted body lives only in memory for the current session and is dropped again on re-lock, auto-lock or when the page closes. There is no recovery path: a forgotten PIN means the ciphertext cannot be opened.

Backups: “Export JSON” creates a file on your device through the browser's download. Locked notes are written to that file exactly as stored — ciphertext, salt and KDF parameters — never as plaintext. Where you keep that file is up to you; the app never sends it anywhere.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads on the tool UI, no in-app purchases, no subscription and no Plus tier. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Feedback: the Guide & Feedback button opens the try-dabble.com hub; anything you send there is covered by the hub's own policy. Nothing from your notes is attached.

Terms of Use

Lockpad is provided as-is as a personal notepad with an optional per-note lock. It is not a password manager, a legal archive or a cloud vault. Notes are only as durable as this browser and any JSON backups you keep yourself.

The lock uses your browser's Web Crypto implementation. A PIN you forget cannot be recovered by anyone, including us — there is no reset, no account and no server copy. Choose a PIN you can remember, and keep a JSON backup.

The service is free, has no ads on the tool UI, no in-app purchases, no subscription, no Plus tier and nothing to cancel. No feature is behind a paywall. It may change or stop without notice. By using the app you agree to these terms.

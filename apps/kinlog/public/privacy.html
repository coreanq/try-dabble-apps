Privacy Policy

Kinlog is local-first. The people you add, their context lines, their notes, the last-contact and next-contact dates and your sort choice are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys kinlog:people:v1, kinlog:sort:v1 and kinlog:open:v1). Language uses localStorage (kinlog:lang) and a td_lang cookie shared across try-dabble.com subdomains. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no payment step, and no server-side copy of your book. Kinlog never requests access to your device's contacts or address book, and it never sends invitations to anyone.

Nothing expires on a timer and an app update never clears your people or resets your next-contact dates. An entry is removed only when you delete it, or when you clear site data in your browser.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Terms of Use

Essaypad is provided as-is as a personal word-progress notebook. It is not an editor, a plagiarism or grading service, or a submission system. Progress and the daily target are only as accurate as the totals you type in; where a word limit matters for a submission, check the count in the tool you write with and follow the rules of the institution or publication.

Everything is stored in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, uninstalling the app or switching devices removes it. Keeping a JSON backup is your responsibility; the export and import buttons are provided for that purpose.

Days left and the daily target are computed from the date on your device. If that date is wrong, the pace will be wrong too.

The service is free, has no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription and nothing to cancel. No essay count or feature is behind a paywall. It may change or stop without notice. By using the app you agree to these terms.

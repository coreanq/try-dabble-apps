Privacy Policy

Fxpad is local-first. The exchange-rate snapshot you sync, your last amount, your From / To currencies and your text-size choice are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys fxpad:rates:v1 and fxpad:prefs:v1). Language uses localStorage (fxpad:lang) and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you type is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your conversions.

Rates provider: When you tap “Refresh rates” (and once on open while online), the app requests https://api.frankfurter.app/latest directly from your browser. That request carries no amount, currency choice or identifier from the app; the provider sees only an ordinary HTTP request from your device. Everything after that happens offline from the saved snapshot.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Clear site data in your browser settings to erase the snapshot and your preferences.

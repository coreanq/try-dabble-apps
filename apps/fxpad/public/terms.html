Offline conversions use the last snapshot you synced. The app shows the date those rates are for; it is your call whether an older snapshot is good enough for the purchase in front of you.

Do not rely on this app for trading, accounting, tax or any decision where an exact rate matters. There is no warranty of accuracy or availability, and the rates provider may change or stop without notice.

Clearing your browser's site data removes the snapshot and your preferences.

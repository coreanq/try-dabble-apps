Terms of Use

Poseguide is provided as-is as a personal photo-posing helper. The pose library, the silhouette overlay, the match score and the voice tips are suggestions, not professional photography or health advice. Only attempt poses that are comfortable and safe for you.

The match score is an estimate made by a small on-device model and will be wrong sometimes, especially in poor light or with loose clothing. It is a nudge, not a judgement.

Photos you capture are saved by your browser to this device. Keep your own backups; the app holds no copy and cannot recover a deleted photo.

Be considerate when using Stranger Mode: you are handing your phone to someone else. Respect people and places you photograph, and follow local rules on photography.

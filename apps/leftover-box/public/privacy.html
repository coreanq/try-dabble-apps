Privacy Policy

Leftover Box is local-first. Dish names, cooked-on dates, eat-by dates, locations, and notes are stored only in this browser (localStorage key leftover-box:items:v1). Language uses localStorage (leftover-box:lang). Nothing is uploaded to our servers for accounts or backup.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server-side stash of your leftovers.

JSON export and import stay on this device.

Advertising: Google AdSense may be active on this site. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure performance. See Google’s advertising policies and your browser or Google account settings to manage ad cookies.

You can export or delete everything in the app, or clear site data in your browser settings.

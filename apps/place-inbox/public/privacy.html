Privacy Policy

Place Inbox is local-first. Places, trip names, why-saved notes, ranks, links, and photos are stored only in this browser (IndexedDB for places, trips, and image blobs). Language and a few UI settings may use localStorage. Nothing is uploaded to our servers for accounts or backup.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server-side stash of your travel wishlist.

The optional map loads OpenStreetMap tiles and Leaflet from a public CDN in your browser. Coordinates you type stay on this device. There is no geocoding service and no location tracking.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

You can export or delete everything in the app, or clear site data in your browser settings.

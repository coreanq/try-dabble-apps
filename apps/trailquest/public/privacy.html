Privacy Policy

Trailquest is local-first. Your custom routes, log entries, milestone progress and preferences (unit, active route, steps per mile, text size) are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys trailquest:routes:v1, trailquest:logs:v1, trailquest:progress:v1 and trailquest:prefs:v1). Language uses localStorage (trailquest:lang) and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you type is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no membership, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your log. The app does not read your phone's step counter, health app or location: every distance is one you typed in. The app makes no network request other than loading its own files.

Backups: “Export JSON” creates a file on your device through the browser’s download. Where you keep that file is up to you; the app never sends it anywhere.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Pictures: every illustration (trail map, milestone markers, icons) is hand-drawn vector art shipped with the app. No generative AI images are used or fetched.

Use “Delete all data” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.

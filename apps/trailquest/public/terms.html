Terms of Use

Trailquest is provided as-is as a personal virtual trail journal. It is a notebook for the distances you type in; it does not record GPS, read a step counter or verify that any distance was walked. Preset route lengths and milestone distances are approximate and for motivation only, not for navigation or trip planning.

Everything is stored in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, uninstalling the app or switching devices removes it. Keeping a JSON backup is your responsibility; the export and import buttons are provided for that purpose.

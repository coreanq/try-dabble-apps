Privacy Policy

Playcue is local-first. Cue names, their order and the current position are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys playcue:cues:v1, playcue:current:v1, playcue:loop-one:v1). Language uses localStorage (playcue:lang) and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

The audio files you add are copied into this device's Origin Private File System so the list still plays after you close and re-open the tab. Those files are never uploaded, never streamed and never read by us.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server-side copy of your show.

Advertising: Google AdSense may be active on this site. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure performance. See Google’s advertising policies and your browser or Google account settings to manage ad cookies.

You can remove any cue in the app, clear the whole list, or clear site data in your browser settings.

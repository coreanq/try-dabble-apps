Privacy Policy

Outcheck is local-first. Your item list, today's calendar day and today's check timestamps are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys outcheck:items, outcheck:day, outcheck:checks). Language uses localStorage (outcheck:lang) and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your checklist. The app does not connect to any lock, sensor or smart-home hub.

Checks clear automatically at local midnight. Item names stay until you remove them.

Advertising: Google AdSense may be active on this site. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure performance. See Google’s advertising policies and your browser or Google account settings to manage ad cookies.

You can remove any item in the app, or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.

Privacy Policy

Localplay is local-first. The files you pick are copied into this browser's private app storage on this device, and the library (titles, artists, albums, favorites, recents), playlists, queue and settings are stored in this browser's local storage under localplay:* keys. Language uses local storage (localplay:lang) and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics and no server-side copy of your music or your library. The app does not read files you have not picked, and it never fetches music from the internet.

The optional JSON export contains track information, playlists, favorites and recents only, never audio. It is written to wherever you choose to save it and is not sent to us.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts, shows nothing during playback, and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Removing a track in the app deletes its copy from this device. Clearing site data in your browser settings erases everything at once; your original files elsewhere on the device are untouched.

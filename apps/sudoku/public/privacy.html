Privacy Policy

3D Sudoku is local-first. The only thing it writes to this device is your chosen UI language (localStorage key sudoku_lang) and a td_lang cookie shared across try-dabble.com subdomains, so language stays consistent when you move between our apps. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

A puzzle in progress — the grid, your notes, undo history and elapsed time — lives only in this page's memory while it is open. Closing the tab or reloading the page does not save it; the next visit starts a fresh puzzle. There is no saved-game list and no puzzle history to look back on.

There are no accounts of any kind: no sign-in, no social login, no email, no payment step and no server-side copy of anything. There is no daily puzzle limit and no premium tier — every board size and difficulty is open every time.

3D Sudoku does not track scores, streaks or completion times across sessions, and keeps no record of how any past game went.

Because there is no server, there is no sync and no shared view: two devices cannot see the same in-progress puzzle.

Optional background music and sound effects, when turned on, are fetched from this site's own assets — never from a third party — and only while the setting is enabled.

3D Sudoku is not a medical device and makes no health claims.

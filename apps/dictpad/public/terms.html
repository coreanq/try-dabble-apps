Dictpad is provided as-is as a personal dictation notepad inside the browser. It is not a system keyboard or input method for other apps, not a transcription service, and not a substitute for a record you are required to keep. Recognised text is only as accurate as your browser’s speech recognition; read it before you rely on it.

Everything is stored in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, uninstalling the app or switching devices removes it. Keeping a JSON or TXT backup is your responsibility; the export and download buttons are provided for that purpose.

Speech recognition is a browser feature and may be unavailable, limited or need an internet connection depending on the browser and device. Typing into the notepad works regardless.

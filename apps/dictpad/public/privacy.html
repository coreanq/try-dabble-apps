Privacy Policy

Dictpad is local-first. Your notes, their titles, the active note and your preferences (recognition language, screen language) stay in this browser on this device. Language uses this browser and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you type or dictate is uploaded to a Dictpad server. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your notes.

Speech recognition: dictation uses your browser’s own speech recognition (the Web Speech API). In most browsers, including Chrome, that service sends the audio to the browser vendor’s speech servers to turn it into text while you are recording, under the browser vendor’s privacy policy. Dictpad never receives that audio and never stores it. When you are not recording, the microphone is off.

Backups: “Export JSON” and “Download .txt” create files on your device through the browser’s download. Where you keep those files is up to you; the app never sends them anywhere.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Use “Delete everything” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.

Privacy Policy

Weekpad is local-first. Your categories, expenses and preferences (currency, week start day, text size) are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys weekpad:categories:v1, weekpad:expenses:v1 and weekpad:prefs:v1). Language uses localStorage (weekpad:lang) and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you type is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no bank or card linking, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your spending. The app makes no network request other than loading its own files.

Backups: “Export JSON” and “Export CSV” create a file on your device through the browser’s download. Where you keep that file is up to you; the app never sends it anywhere.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Use “Delete all data” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.

Privacy Policy

Playset is local-first. The playlists you build — their names, the games in them and the order they play in — are stored only in this browser (localStorage key playset:playlists:v1). Where you got to in a running queue is stored the same way (playset:session:v1) so a locked phone or a closed tab comes back to the same game. Language uses localStorage (playset:lang) and a td_lang cookie shared across try-dabble.com subdomains. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

There are no accounts of any kind: no sign-in, no social login, no email, no payment step and no server-side copy of anything you make. There is no three-games-a-day limit, no premium tier and no trial that ends — all six games open every time.

Playset does not measure anyone. It keeps no score history, no percentile, no comparison against other people and no record of how any round went. The row of suns you see during a game is drawn from memory for that game only and is gone the moment the game ends.

Because there is no server, there is no sync and no shared view: two devices cannot see the same playlists.

Nothing expires on a timer. A playlist is removed only when you remove it, or when you clear site data in your browser.

Playset is not a medical device, a diagnosis, a treatment or a cognitive assessment. It makes no health claims.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

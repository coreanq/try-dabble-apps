Terms of Use

Listenpad is provided as-is as a personal listening-practice player inside the browser. It plays audio files you already have on this device; it does not provide, download or transcribe audio, and it is not a music library or a substitute for any course material. Use it only with audio you are allowed to play.

Settings, loop points and notes are stored in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, uninstalling the app or switching devices removes them. Keeping a JSON backup is your responsibility; the export button is provided for that purpose. Audio files are never stored by the app and are not part of a backup.

Which formats play, and whether a file stays open after a reload, depends on the browser and device. On iPhone you may need to pick the file again.

The service is free, has no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription and nothing to cancel. It may change or stop without notice. By using the app you agree to these terms.

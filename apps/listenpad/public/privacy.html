Privacy Policy

Listenpad is local-first. The audio file you pick is opened straight from this device by your browser and played here; it is never uploaded to a Listenpad server or anywhere else. Your settings (rewind amount, speed, A–B loop points, the last file name) and your listening notes stay in this browser on this device. Language uses this browser and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your audio, settings or notes.

Backups: “Export JSON” creates a file on your device through the browser’s download. It contains settings and notes only, never audio. Where you keep that file is up to you; the app never sends it anywhere.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. Rewind, A–B loop and speed are free. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Use “Delete everything” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything. Your audio files are never touched.

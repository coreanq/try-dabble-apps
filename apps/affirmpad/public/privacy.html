Privacy Policy

Affirmpad is local-first. Your affirmations, topics, practice counts and preferences (dark mode, sound, vibration, text size, session goal) are stored only in this browser (localStorage keys affirmpad:affirmations:v1, affirmpad:categories:v1, affirmpad:prefs:v1 and affirmpad:session:v1). Language uses localStorage (affirmpad:lang) and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you write is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your affirmations. The app makes no network request other than loading its own files.

Sound and vibration: the tap click is generated in the browser and the vibration uses the device’s own vibration API. Neither records anything or leaves the device.

Backups: “Export JSON” creates a file on your device through the browser’s download. Where you keep that file is up to you; the app never sends it anywhere.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Use “Delete all data” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.

Terms of Use

Storelog is a free browser tool provided as-is, without warranty of any kind.

Your stores, numbers and notes live in this browser only. They are not backed up anywhere, so clearing site data, switching devices, or losing the device loses them. Export a CSV or JSON copy if this is a list you cannot lose.

Storelog does not call anyone, does not verify a store number, and does not look anything up. It stores the text you type. Whether a number still reaches that store is between you and the store.

The tool may change or stop working at any time. Google AdSense ads may appear.

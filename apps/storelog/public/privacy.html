Privacy Policy

Storelog is local-first. The stores you add — the name, the store number or phone number, and the notes — are stored only in this browser (localStorage key storelog:stores:v1). Language uses localStorage (storelog:lang) and a td_lang cookie shared across try-dabble.com subdomains. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no payment step, and no server-side copy of your list. There is no record limit and no paid tier: the app does not stop at 100 entries and does not lock anything behind a PRO upgrade.

Export is local too. The JSON and CSV files are built inside this page and handed straight to your browser's download — they are never sent anywhere first.

Nothing expires on a timer. An entry is removed only when you remove it, or when you clear site data in your browser.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

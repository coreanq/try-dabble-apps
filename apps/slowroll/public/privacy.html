Privacy Policy

Slowroll is local-first. Every frame you shoot or add is stored only in this browser's IndexedDB on this device. Roll state, the develop mode and the absolute unlock time are kept in localStorage (slowroll:rolls:v1, slowroll:view:v1). Language uses localStorage (slowroll:lang) and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

No image, roll, or timestamp is ever uploaded, streamed, or read by us. The camera stream is used only to draw a frame into local storage when you press the shutter; it is never recorded or sent anywhere.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server-side copy of your rolls.

Advertising: This app has no ads. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

You can discard a whole roll in the app, or clear site data in your browser settings to remove everything.

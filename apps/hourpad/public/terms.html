Terms of Use

Hourpad is provided as-is as a personal work-hours notebook. It is not a payroll system, an employer’s time-and-attendance record or legal advice. Totals and the overtime bank are only as accurate as your check-ins, check-outs and manual entries; where hours matter for pay or compliance, keep whatever record your employer or contract requires.

Everything is stored in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, uninstalling the app or switching devices removes it. Keeping a JSON backup is your responsibility; the export and import buttons are provided for that purpose.

Elapsed time is computed from the clock on your device. If that clock is wrong, the hours will be wrong too.

The service is free, has no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription and nothing to cancel. Breaks and the overtime bank are not behind any paywall. It may change or stop without notice. By using the app you agree to these terms.

Privacy Policy

Hourpad is local-first. Your work and break sessions, task labels, the open session, your weekly target, the overtime bank balance and the settled-week ledger stay in this browser on this device. Language uses this browser and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you enter is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no company or team workspace, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your hours. Nobody but you can see when you checked in or out.

Backups: “Export JSON” creates a file on your device through the browser’s download. Where you keep that file is up to you; the app never sends it anywhere.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. Breaks and the overtime bank are free. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Use “Delete everything” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.

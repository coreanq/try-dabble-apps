Privacy Policy

Recpad is local-first. The audio you record, every edit, the noise clean, the drafts you save and your settings stay in this browser on this device. Recording, noise clean, gain and export all run inside the browser tab; the app makes no network request for any of them. Language uses this browser and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you record is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics and no server-side copy of your audio. The microphone is only opened while you are recording and is released when you press Stop. After the page and its own files have loaded, the only outside script is the shared try-dabble feedback widget, which cannot hear or read your audio.

Downloads: “Download WAV” and “Download MP3” create files on your device through the browser’s download. Where you keep or share those files is up to you; the app never sends them anywhere.

Drafts: “Save as draft” keeps the take in this browser so it is still there next time you open the app, even after a refresh. Delete a draft in the app, or clear site data in your browser settings, to remove it.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. Noise clean and export are free. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

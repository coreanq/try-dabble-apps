Terms of Use

Progresspad is provided as-is as a personal progress notebook. It is not a time-tracking system of record, a billing tool or a study-planning service. Progress is only as accurate as the minutes you log by hand or save from the optional timer; where hours matter for pay or credit, keep the record your employer or institution requires.

Everything is stored in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, uninstalling the app or switching devices removes it. Keeping a JSON backup is your responsibility; the export and import buttons are provided for that purpose.

The optional timer and the default log date use the clock on your device. If that clock is wrong, the minutes and dates will be wrong too.

The service is free, has no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription, no Plus tier and nothing to cancel. No task count or feature is behind a paywall. It may change or stop without notice. By using the app you agree to these terms.

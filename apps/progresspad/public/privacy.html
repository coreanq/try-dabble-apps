Privacy Policy

Progresspad is local-first. Your tasks, their targets in hours or percent, every focus log you enter (date, minutes, note), the optional timer's start time and the stage preference stay in this browser on this device. Language uses this browser and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you enter is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server-side copy of your progress. The app never knows what you were working on beyond the title you typed.

Backups: “Export JSON” creates a file on your device through the browser’s download. Where you keep that file is up to you; the app never sends it anywhere.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription and no Plus tier. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Use “Delete everything” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.

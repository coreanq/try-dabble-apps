Privacy Policy

Scrubpad is local-first. The text you paste, the detections, the scrubbed result, your custom words and your settings stay in this browser on this device. Scrubbing runs inside the browser tab; the app makes no network request for it. Language uses this browser and the shared td_lang cookie on try-dabble.com.

Nothing you paste is uploaded. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics and no server-side copy of your text. After the page and its own files have loaded, the only outside script is the shared try-dabble feedback widget, which does not read what you paste.

Downloads: “Download scrubbed”, “Download restore map” and “Export dictionary” create files on your device through the browser’s download. The restore map contains the original values, so keep it on this device and out of any chat. Where you keep those files is up to you; the app never sends them anywhere.

Last paste: when “Keep last paste on this device” is on, the last text you pasted is kept in this browser so it is still there next time. Turn it off on a shared device, or use “Delete everything”.

Advertising and payments: This app has no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. It loads no advertising scripts and sets no third-party advertising cookies. The only cookie is td_lang, which remembers your language across try-dabble.com apps.

Use “Delete everything” in the app or clear site data in your browser settings to erase everything.
